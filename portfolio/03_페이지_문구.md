### cbm-pathology-ssl

#### title

A Controlled Comparison of Self-Supervised Design Choices with Limited Labels for Colon H&E Classification:\nNCT-CRC Training and CRC-VAL External Validation

#### authors

Jaemin Hwang, Meen Hye Lee

#### venue

Computers in Biology and Medicine (투고 대상, 미투고)

#### year

2026

#### status

draft

#### featured

2

#### abstract

Colon H&E patch classification must contend with costly expert annotation and distribution shift across sites and stains. A practical question is therefore whether models fine-tuned with limited labels transfer to an external cohort. We do not claim a new color curriculum. Instead, we run a controlled comparison on a held-out NCT validation set and a CRC-VAL test set, varying only selected design choices, and record how external accuracy changes. In particular, we ask whether scheduled augmentation outperforms plain SSL in the low-label regime, and whether scheduled color emphasis yields a color-specific gain. We pretrain ResNet-18 with SimCLR-style SSL (40 epochs) on NCT-CRC-HE-100K-NONORM, fine-tune, and report 9-class accuracy on CRC-VAL-HE-7K as the sole external metric. The grid covers seven tags, label fractions 10%/25%/100%, and seeds 42, 43, and 44 (63 runs). Scheduled augmentation settings (curriculum or schedule_colorless) outperform training from scratch at every label fraction, and at 10% and 25% also outperform plain SSL (none) in mean accuracy (10% curriculum 0.562 vs. none 0.321; 25% colorless 0.594 vs. none 0.282). The curriculum vs. colorless gap is small, so a color-specific gain is not supported. fixed_emphasize (p = 1 throughout SSL pretraining) is not a beneficial setting at any of the three fractions. Across the three label fractions, the curriculum mean stays near 0.562, so adding labels does not raise it. As a sensitivity check, macenko_none (per-image rescaling at test time; Sec. 3.3) and ImageNet initialization obtain higher mean accuracy than curriculum, but the former uses a different test input and is not ranked with the training settings (Sec. 2.4). The main findings are the low-label schedule vs. plain SSL observation and the lack of evidence for a color-specific gain. Under this protocol, raising CRC-VAL accuracy is more sensitive to the evaluation pipeline and initialization than to curriculum.

#### keywords

self-supervised learning, digital pathology, label efficiency, external validation, controlled comparison, colorectal histopathology, held-out validation

#### summary

대장 H&E 패치 분류는 전문가 라벨링 비용이 크고, 기관과 염색 방식에 따른 분포 차이도 큽니다.\n이 연구는 새로운 방법을 내세우기보다, 라벨이 적을 때 self-supervised 설계 선택이 외부 코호트 성능을 어떻게 바꾸는지 통제된 조건에서 비교합니다.\nNCT-CRC-HE-100K-NONORM으로 ResNet-18을 SimCLR 방식으로 사전학습하고, 7개 설정 × 라벨 비율 10·25·100% × 시드 3개, 총 63회 실험의 CRC-VAL-HE-7K 9-class 정확도를 비교했습니다.\n스케줄 증강 설정은 모든 라벨 비율에서 scratch 학습보다 좋았고, 10%와 25%에서는 일반 SSL보다도 평균 정확도가 높았습니다(10% curriculum 0.562 vs. 0.321).\n다만 curriculum과 colorless의 차이는 작아 색 고유의 이득은 뒷받침되지 않았고, CRC-VAL 정확도는 curriculum보다 평가 파이프라인과 초기화에 더 민감했습니다.\n\n
Computers in Biology and Medicine 투고를 준비하고 있습니다.\n한국어 초고를 마쳤고, 영문 원고를 다듬고 있습니다.

### ksmi-2026-wearable-colormap

#### title

Color-Encoding Shortcuts in Wearable Time-Series CNNs: A Public-Data Audit

#### authors

Jaemin Hwang, Meen Hye Lee

#### venue

2026 Fall Conference of the Korean Society of Medical Informatics

#### year

2026

#### status

submitted

#### featured

1

#### abstract

Time-series-to-image CNNs require a colormap when real-valued matrices become RGB images, yet Methods seldom name it. We audited performance effects, shortcut dependence, and reporting of this choice on two public wearable datasets. With a Gramian Angular Summation Field and scratch-trained ResNet-18, we classified multimorbidity from NHANES 2011–2014 wrist accelerometry and resting versus ambulatory activity from UCI PAMAP2, comparing viridis, jet, and gray. A label-tied positive control added a red border only on positive-class images; the same weights were then scored with the border aligned, removed, or reversed. We counted colormap naming in a convenience sample of 40 Gramian Angular Field, Markov Transition Field, or recurrence plot papers with a CNN. The maximum gap in test AUROC among conventional colormaps was 0.028 on NHANES and 0.018 on PAMAP2. For these datasets, encoding, and architecture, evidence that conventional colormap choice materially changes in-distribution AUROC is limited, and we claim no equivalence. Aligned test AUROC was 1.000. Removing the border dropped NHANES test AUROC to about 0.45, near chance; reversing it yielded 0.000 on both datasets. Seven papers (17.5%) named a colormap. The choice is poorly documented, and the model can become strongly dependent on an explicitly label-tied color cue. These results suggest locking and reporting the colormap and keeping label-tied color out of the image.

#### keywords

colormap, shortcut learning, wearable accelerometry

#### summary

시계열을 이미지로 바꿔 CNN에 넣을 때는 colormap을 반드시 골라야 하지만, 논문의 방법 절에는 거의 적히지 않습니다.\nNHANES 2011–2014 손목 가속도계 데이터(다중이환 분류)와 UCI PAMAP2(휴식·보행 활동 분류)를 GASF 이미지로 바꾸고, ResNet-18로 viridis, jet, gray를 비교했습니다.\n일반적인 colormap 사이의 test AUROC 차이는 최대 0.028(NHANES)과 0.018(PAMAP2)로, colormap 선택이 성능을 크게 바꾼다는 근거는 제한적이었습니다.\n반면 양성 이미지에만 빨간 테두리를 넣은 대조 실험에서는 AUROC가 1.000이었고, 테두리를 없애면 NHANES에서 약 0.45로, 뒤집으면 0.000으로 떨어져 모델이 색 단서에 강하게 의존할 수 있음을 확인했습니다.\n관련 논문 40편 중 colormap을 명시한 논문은 7편(17.5%)뿐이어서, colormap을 고정해 보고하고 라벨과 연결된 색은 이미지에서 배제할 것을 제안합니다.\n\n
대한의료정보학회 2026 추계학술대회에 투고해 심사를 받고 있습니다.

### ckaia-2026-cielab

#### title

Achieving Label Efficiency in Medical Image Classification through CIELAB Chroma Emphasis

#### authors

Jaemin Hwang

#### venue

CKAIA 2026

#### year

2026

#### status

accepted

#### abstract

In medical image classification, color often serves as a diagnostic cue, yet the acquisition of large-scale labeled data requires substantial expert annotation cost and time. This paper leverages the tendency of convolutional neural networks to rely on superficial patterns and proposes lab ab, an input preprocessing method that emphasizes the chroma channels in the CIELAB color space, to examine whether performance comparable to a full-data baseline, that is, label efficiency, can be attained with reduced training data. The study begins with an exploration of low-pass and saturation preprocessing, establishes lab ab as the proposed method on HAM10000 with ResNet-18, and then performs generalization and external validation on MobileNetV2, LC25000, and BreakHis. When ResNet-18 is trained from scratch on HAM10000, the test macro-F1 of lab ab at a 50 percent train ratio, 0.430, is similar to the 100 percent train ratio baseline, 0.448, within an absolute tolerance of 0.03. The same pattern is reproduced on MobileNetV2. On the LC25000 histopathology data, label efficiency holds nominally, but the effect is marginal because the task difficulty is low. On the BreakHis breast histopathology data, label efficiency was not reproduced under the 50 percent train slide condition of about 600 patches, but at 100 percent train slides lab ab reached an F1 of 0.302 and exceeded the baseline of 0.180. This suggests that non-reproduction stems from insufficient training data scale rather than a fundamental limitation of the method. Focusing on dermatoscopy, where color is diagnostically important, lab ab demonstrates that input preprocessing can improve label efficiency without changing the existing CNN training procedure.

#### keywords

CNN, CIELAB, Chroma Emphasis, Label Efficiency, Medical Image Classification

#### summary

의료 영상에서 색은 중요한 진단 단서지만, 대규모 라벨 데이터를 만들려면 전문가의 시간과 비용이 많이 듭니다.\n이 연구는 CNN이 표면적인 패턴에 기대는 성질을 활용해, CIELAB 색 공간의 chroma 채널을 강조하는 입력 전처리 lab ab를 제안했습니다.\nHAM10000에서 ResNet-18을 처음부터 학습했을 때, 학습 데이터 50%만 쓴 lab ab의 macro-F1(0.430)이 전체 데이터 기준선(0.448)과 0.03 이내로 비슷했고, MobileNetV2에서도 같은 경향이 재현됐습니다.\nLC25000에서는 과제가 쉬워 효과가 미미했고, BreakHis에서는 50% 조건에서 재현되지 않았지만 100% 조건에서 F1 0.302로 기준선 0.180을 넘었습니다.\n기존 CNN 학습 절차를 바꾸지 않고 입력 전처리만으로 라벨 효율을 높일 수 있음을 보였습니다.\n\n

### pub-earthquake-kips

#### title

Stochastic Self-similarity Analysis and Visualization of Earthquakes on the Korean Peninsula

#### authors

Jaemin Hwang, Jiyoung Lim, and Hae-Duck J. Jeong

#### venue

KIPS Transactions on Software and Data Engineering, vol. 12, no. 11, pp. 493–504, 2023. KCI

#### year

2023

#### status

published

#### abstract

The Republic of Korea is located far from the boundary of the earthquake plate, and the intra-plate earthquake occurring in these areas is generally small in size and less frequent than the interplate earthquake. Nevertheless, as a result of investigating and analyzing earthquakes that occurred on the Korean Peninsula between the past two years and 1904 and earthquakes that occurred after observing recent earthquakes on the Korean Peninsula, earthquakes of magnitude up to 9 were found. In this paper, the Korean Peninsula Historical Earthquake Record (2 years to 1904) published by the National Meteorological Research Institute is used to analyze the relationship between earthquakes on the Korean Peninsula and statistical self-similarity. In addition, the problem solved through this paper was the first to investigate the relationship between earthquake data occurring on the Korean Peninsula and statistical self-similarity. As a result of measuring the degree of self-similarity of earthquakes on the Korean Peninsula using three quantitative estimation methods, the self-similarity parameter H value (0.5 < H < 1) was found to be above 0.8 on average, indicating a high degree of self-similarity. And through graph visualization, it can be easily figured out in which region earthquakes occur most often, and it is expected that it can be used in the development of a prediction system that can predict damage in the event of an earthquake in the future and minimize damage to property and people, as well as in earthquake data analysis and modeling research. Based on the findings of this study, the self-similar process is expected to help understand the patterns and statistical characteristics of seismic activities, group and classify similar seismic events, and be used for prediction of seismic activities, seismic risk assessments, and seismic engineering.

#### keywords

Earthquake, Stochastic Self-similarity, Visualization, Self-similar Process, Hurst Parameter

#### summary

한반도는 판 경계에서 멀리 떨어져 있어 판 내부 지진이 주로 발생하며, 판 경계 지진보다 규모와 빈도가 작은 편입니다.\n이 연구는 국립기상연구소의 「한반도 역사지진 기록(2년~1904년)」을 이용해, 한반도 지진 데이터와 통계적 자기 유사성의 관계를 처음으로 분석했습니다.\n세 가지 정량적 추정 방법으로 자기 유사성 파라미터 H를 측정한 결과, 평균 0.8 이상으로 자기 유사성이 높게 나타났습니다.\n그래프 시각화로 지진이 자주 발생한 지역을 한눈에 파악할 수 있게 했습니다.\n이 결과는 지진 피해 예측 시스템, 지진 데이터 분석·모델링, 지진 위험 평가 연구에 활용될 수 있습니다.\n\n

### pub-crosswalk-applsci

#### title

CNN-Based Crosswalk Pedestrian Situation Recognition System Using Mask-R-CNN and CDA

#### authors

Sac Lee, Jaemin Hwang, Junbeom Kim, and Jinho Han

#### venue

Applied Sciences, 13(7): 4291, Apr. 2023. SCIE

#### year

2023

#### status

published

#### links

DOI https://doi.org/10.3390/app13074291

#### abstract

Researchers are studying CNN (convolutional neural networks) in various ways for image classification. Sometimes, they must classify two or more objects in an image into different situations according to their location. We developed a new learning method that colored objects from images and extracted them to distinguish the relationship between objects with different colors. We can apply this method in certain situations, such as pedestrians in a crosswalk. This paper presents a method for learning pedestrian situations on CNN using Mask R-CNN (Region-based CNN) and CDA (Crosswalk Detection Algorithm). With this method, we classified the location of the pedestrians into two situations: safety and danger. We organized the process of preprocessing and learning images into three stages. In Stage 1, we used Mask R-CNN to detect pedestrians. In Stage 2, we detected crosswalks with the CDA and placed colors on detected objects. In Stage 3, we combined crosswalks and pedestrian objects into one image and then, learned the image to CNN. We trained ResNet50 and Xception using images in the proposed method and evaluated the accuracy of the results. When tested experimentally, ResNet50 exhibited 96.7% accuracy and Xception showed 98.7% accuracy. We then created an image that simplified the situation with two colored boxes of crosswalks and pedestrians. We confirmed that the learned CNN with the images of colored boxes could classify the same test images applied in the previous experiment with 96% accuracy by ResNet50. This result indicates that the proposed system is suitable for classifying pedestrian safety and dangerous situations by accurately dividing the positions of the two objects.

#### keywords

Efficient Large Dataset Construction using Image Smoothing and Image Size Reduction

#### authors

#### venue

Korean Journal of Artificial Intelligence, 11(1), pp. 17–24, Mar. 2023. KCI

#### year

#### status

published

#### abstract

With the continuous growth in the amount of data collected and analyzed, deep learning has become increasingly popular for extracting meaningful insights from various fields. However, hardware limitations pose a challenge for achieving meaningful results with limited data. To address this challenge, this paper proposes an algorithm that leverages the characteristics of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to reduce the size of image datasets by 20% through smoothing and shrinking the size of images using color elements. The proposed algorithm reduces the learning time and, as a result, the computational load on hardware. The experiments conducted in this study show that the proposed method achieves effective learning with similar or slightly higher accuracy than the original dataset while reducing computational and time costs. This color-centric dataset construction method using image smoothing techniques can lead to more efficient learning on CNNs. This method can be applied in various applications, such as image classification and recognition, and can contribute to more efficient and cost-effective deep learning. This paper presents a promising approach to reducing the computational load and time costs associated with deep learning and provides meaningful results with limited data, enabling them to apply deep learning to a broader range of applications.

### pub-crosswalk-icaeic

#### title

Crosswalk Pedestrian Situation Recognition System

#### authors

Sac Lee, Jaemin Hwang, and Jinho Han

#### venue

ICAEIC 2022, pp. 34–37, Jul. 2022

#### year

2022

#### status

published

#### bestPaper

예

#### abstract

The proportion of seriousness in pedestrian traffic accidents has been known about 17%, indicating that pedestrians are not safe in traffic conditions. Identifying the location of pedestrians at crosswalks is important to protect the safety of pedestrians. In this paper, a new method is proposed for grasping situations according to the location of pedestrians using Mask R-CNN and CNN. The overall process of the method consists of data stage and training-test stage. In data stage, the method detects pedestrians and crosswalks of the original images with Mask R-CNN and performs image processing properly for the next stage. In training-test stage, the method determines pedestrian situations using CNN models for images processed during data stage. Testing accuracy of CNN model trained by the proposed method showed 98% accuracy in our experiments. Therefore, our method can be said to grasp the situation of pedestrians between pedestrian and crosswalk.

#### keywords

Mask R-CNN, crosswalk Pedestrian, CNN, grasp situation

#### summary

보행자 교통사고 중 심각한 사고의 비율이 약 17%로 알려져 있어, 횡단보도에서 보행자의 위치를 파악하는 일이 중요합니다.\n이 연구는 Mask R-CNN과 CNN을 이용해 보행자의 위치에 따른 상황을 파악하는 방법을 제안했습니다.\n데이터 단계에서는 Mask R-CNN으로 원본 이미지의 보행자와 횡단보도를 검출하고, 다음 단계에 맞게 이미지를 처리했습니다.\n학습·테스트 단계에서는 처리된 이미지로 CNN 모델이 보행자 상황을 판단하도록 했습니다.\n제안한 방법으로 학습한 CNN은 실험에서 98%의 정확도를 보였습니다.\n\n
